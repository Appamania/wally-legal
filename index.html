What's inside

  • 90 puzzles across 6 worlds: Backyard, Kitchen, Rooftops, Laundromat, Subway and Space Fair. All free.
  • Learn each new tile by playing it. No tutorial screens.
  • Free, unlimited Undo. There is no fail state, only better routes.
  • Daily Walk: one shared puzzle a day, with a streak and a leaderboard.
  • Rush: 60 seconds of fast numbers.
  • Caps for Wally. He pretends not to care.
  • Plays offline. No account needed.

Free, and fair about it

Every level is free. When you are stuck, the first hint in each world is free, and Bolts you earn by playing buy more. If you run out, you can watch a short ad for a hint, but only when you ask for one. Wally Plus is a one-time purchase: free hints with no ads, the Founder cap and 500 Bolts.

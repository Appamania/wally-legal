Privacy Policy

Effective 6 October 2026

Wally is a game made by Appamania ("we"). This policy explains what information the game uses, why, and the choices you have. In short: there are no accounts with us, we run no servers that store your data, and the only data that leaves your device goes to Google (ads, crash reports, new levels) and to Apple or Google (purchases, leaderboards, and your own cloud backup).

Your progress

Your progress (levels, stars, Bolts, caps, daily streak, best scores) and settings are saved on your device. On iPhone and iPad, a copy is also kept in your own iCloud account, so it comes back after a reinstall or on a new device signed in to the same Apple ID. On Android, Google's app backup keeps a copy of the save file in your Google account if backup is on. We never receive either copy.

Settings → Delete my data clears your progress on the device and replaces the iCloud copy. Your Wally Plus purchase stays, because it belongs to your store account.

What we do not collect

No name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location. There is no sign-up and no account with us.

Ads, tracking and consent

Where the law requires it (for example in the EU, UK and Switzerland), the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, using Google's consent form. On iPhone and iPad, the game asks for permission to track through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you decline, ads are not personalised and the game works the same.

Children

Wally is suitable for everyone but is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided information through the game, contact us and we will help remove it.

How long data is kept

Data on your device stays until you delete it or the app. Cloud backups stay until you delete them in your iCloud or Google account. Leaderboard scores are kept by Apple or Google under their own terms. Crash reports are kept by Google for up to 90 days.

Your choices and rights

  • Limit ad tracking in your device's privacy settings, or switch ads off with Wally Plus.
  • Clear your progress with Settings → Delete my data.
  • Stop sharing scores by signing out of Game Center or Google Play Games; the game works the same.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data, or to object to its use. We hold no personal data on our own servers, so most data can only be linked to you by Google or Apple; contact us and we will help with any request.

Security

Data sent to Google, Apple and the app stores travels over encrypted connections. Your progress is protected by your device's own security, and cloud copies by your Apple ID or Google account.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

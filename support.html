Support

Help with the game, purchases and your progress.

I bought Wally Plus. How do I get it back?

Open Settings → Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account you bought it with. On Android it usually comes back on its own at launch.

I deleted the game. Is my progress gone?

Usually not. On iPhone and iPad, your progress is kept in your iCloud account and comes back when you reinstall with the same Apple ID and iCloud on. On Android, Google's app backup brings it back when you reinstall with the same account and backup on.

A level feels impossible

Every level is checked by a solver to be winnable. Undo is free and unlimited, so try a different first move. Long-press a tile next to Wally to preview his number before you step. Tap Hint to see the next best step.

Leaderboards

Sign in to Game Center (iPhone) or Google Play Games (Android) to see how your Daily Walk and Daily Rush scores rank. The game works the same without signing in.

Ads

Wally never shows an ad on its own. In the hint menu you can choose to watch one in exchange for a hint. Wally Plus switches ads off and makes every hint free.
